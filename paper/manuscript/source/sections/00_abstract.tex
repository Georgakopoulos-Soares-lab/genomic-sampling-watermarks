\begin{abstract}
We study secret-key sampling watermarks for genomic language models that generate fixed 6-mer DNA tokens. The verifier receives only a DNA sequence, a key, and public configuration. Our study treats Carbon and GENERator-v2 as complementary generation systems, measures their realized watermark channel, and evaluates detection after nucleotide edits with calibration over strand, phase, window, and key-offset search. This manuscript is a protocol scaffold; empirical claims will be inserted only from the admitted evidence ledger.
\end{abstract}
