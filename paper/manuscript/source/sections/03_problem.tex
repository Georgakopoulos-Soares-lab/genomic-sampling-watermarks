\section{Problem and Threat Model}

At generation step $t$, let $P_t$ be the declared distribution over canonical 6-mer tokens. A keyed sampler emits $X_t$. The generator has model access and a secret key. The standalone verifier receives DNA, the key, and public configuration, but not the prompt, model, logits, generation seed, or trace.

The detector may search both strand orientations, all six fixed-block phases, and preregistered windows and key offsets. Its reported false-positive rate is calibrated for the maximum statistic over this complete procedure.
