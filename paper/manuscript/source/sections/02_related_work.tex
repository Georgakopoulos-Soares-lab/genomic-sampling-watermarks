\section{Related Work}

Distribution-preserving language-model watermarks include inverse-transform and exponential sampling constructions \cite{kuditipudi2023robust} and unbiased reweighting methods \cite{hu2024unbiased}. Their robustness and security guarantees depend on the precise sampling, key-use, and adversarial setting. We distinguish one-output marginal preservation from stronger multi-query notions.

Carbon \cite{carbon2026} and GENERator-v2 \cite{generatorv22026} provide the fixed 6-mer genomic generation settings considered here. Prior biological-sequence watermarking motivates the domain but does not resolve the direct-token versus base-marginal channel or fixed-block resynchronization problem.
